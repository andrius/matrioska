# CLAUDE.md

## What this is
A fork of the Adhearsion `matrioska` plugin (Ruby gem) for running background
in-call voice apps - listen for keypresses during a call and trigger actions
(transfer, start/stop recording). Remote `github.com/andrius/matrioska`
(fork of adhearsion/matrioska). Reference/fork, not original work.

## Structure
- `lib/` (gem code), `spec/` (RSpec), `matrioska.gemspec`, `Rakefile`.

## Working here
Standard Ruby gem. Upstream fork - prefer upstream over local changes.
